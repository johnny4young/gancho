# Gancho v0.10.0 — Reuse more, with less in the way

Gancho 0.10.0 adds reviewed ways to reuse what you copied: text recipes,
optional meaning suggestions, selected context for AI tools, and translation
with the languages already installed on your Mac. The panel opens solid by
default, starts on your newest clip, and keeps developer actions one click
away. The Library stays current while it is open, and an app-wide review
tightens privacy, sync, contrast and keyboard handling.

## Highlights

- **Text recipes.** Chain up to eight deterministic steps (clean OCR text,
  dedupe lines, redact personal data) and preview the result before copying.
  Recipes are encrypted on the Mac and never synced; three presets ship, and
  deleted presets can be restored.
- **Meaning suggestions, off by default.** Turn on **By meaning** in the panel
  to add a separate "Related by meaning" section under your search. Regular
  search results are unchanged. Suggestions draw on the on-device semantic
  index, which is built only with Pro; on Free the panel reports that clips are
  not indexed.
- **Selected context for AI.** Select clips and prepare them as reviewed,
  bounded Markdown, or grant an AI tool read-only access to exactly those clips
  for one hour. A clip that changes after review stops being served.
- **Translation with installed languages.** On macOS 26, Translate uses
  Apple's on-device Translation framework for language pairs already
  installed, even without Apple Intelligence.
- **A calmer panel.** The panel is solid by default (Settings › General ›
  Translucent background brings the glass back), every open starts on the
  newest clip with the search field focused, arrows move without a cross-fade,
  and developer actions sit behind **More actions**, which is remembered.
- **A Library and panel that keep up.** The open Library shows captures, pins,
  board changes and edits made in the panel without reopening, keeps the pages
  you already scrolled through, and offers Retry when a read fails. Scrolling
  no longer redraws every thumbnail, and a copy no longer re-indexes Spotlight.
  Text on accent-coloured buttons stays readable, icon buttons have larger
  labelled targets, the board picker toggles several boards with Return, and a
  snippet with many fields keeps Insert and Cancel visible at large text sizes.
- **Privacy and sync hardening.** Expired clips disappear everywhere at once,
  metadata-only MCP access can no longer search clip bodies, deletions made
  while sync is off no longer return, images and semantic vectors are removed
  with their clip, and the `gancho` CLI refuses to save or copy secrets
  unless you ask it to. A board you delete stays deleted while sync is still
  sending the deletion, clips that still list it are corrected on iCloud right
  away, a clip edited while its upload is prepared is sent with the text and
  details of one edit, a queued deletion survives an older upload conflict or
  automatic retry, a preview or thumbnail that becomes protected while it loads
  is never shown, and credit cards are detected inside mixed text.
- **Safer storage, backups and licensing.** A failed backup export never
  damages the previous backup, a malformed Keychain key fails safely instead of
  being replaced, store recovery sets aside the whole database generation
  together, a Pro license can be re-activated after signing out without
  restarting Gancho, and CSV import keeps CRLF records, combining marks and
  quoted newlines. SQLCipher moves to 4.19.0 and Sparkle to 2.10.0.

## Install or update

Download the [signed DMG](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.10.0/Gancho-0.10.0.dmg)
and its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.10.0/Gancho-0.10.0.dmg.sha256).
Compare the downloaded bytes with the sidecar before installing. Existing direct
installs can use the signed Sparkle update; Homebrew's cask follows the published
artifact. If assets or the update feed are not available yet, publication is not
complete—do not substitute an older candidate from another source.

Gancho requires macOS 15.4 or later; the on-device model tier, translation
with installed languages and Liquid Glass additionally require macOS 26. The
iPhone and iPad targets require iOS 26 or later and are not distributed by the
DMG or Homebrew cask.

## Availability

Local history remains free. Pro is a separate direct purchase and unlocks the
documented higher limits, on-device intelligence, and encrypted iCloud sync.
The App Store and TestFlight channels remain in preparation.

## Known limitations

- Text recognized in images (OCR) stays on the Mac that recognized it; it is
  not synced to your other devices.
- Backups made with Settings › General › Back up history… do not include
  boards, board membership or text recipes yet. Recipes are also never synced,
  so a recipe you created exists only on the Mac where you made it.
- Back up history… replaces an existing folder only when it is a previous
  Gancho backup or is empty; pick a new name for any other folder.
- This DMG distributes macOS only. iPhone/iPad installation requires a separate
  distribution path; App Store and TestFlight availability is not implied.
