# Gancho v0.9.0 — A redesigned panel, with the same privacy

Gancho 0.9.0 redesigns the Mac history panel. One toolbar instead of three,
rows that show what each clip is at a glance, a peek that opens with a hero
card and closes with an action dock, motion that respects Reduce Motion, an
optional ambient colour and a gallery layout behind ⌘G. Nothing about what the
panel reads, stores or sends changes: links are parsed on your Mac and never
fetched, Private Mode still hides content, and protected clips stay protected.

## Highlights

- **One toolbar, not three.** Boards on the left, type filters as glyph chips
  that expand to their name when active, the source-app filter and Save filter
  on the right. Compact panels wrap the toolbar without clipping controls, and
  keyboard navigation scrolls boards into view. The search field sits directly
  on the panel glass at a larger size, with autocorrection and text suggestions
  off for literal queries.
- **Every clip is recognisable.** A link's host initial, the real colour
  swatch or the image thumbnail sits in the row tile; code previews are
  syntax-tinted; the source app and a minute-granular capture time sit in a
  trailing column. iPhone and iPad history rows share the same layout.
- **A peek that acts.** It opens with a hero card per kind — the image with
  its Live-Text regions, a link's host and complete URL, a colour band, or the
  text — and closes with a dock: Paste, Paste plain, Copy text from image, Pin
  and Board, each with its key. Return still pastes and ↑↓ still walk the
  actions in the same order. The insight line names the boards a clip is in.
- **Motion with restraint.** The panel settles in on open, the peek swaps with
  a soft blur, the active board and type chip glide to your choice, rows and
  actions light up under the pointer, and the pin glyph bounces once when
  toggled. Everything is instant when Reduce Motion is on.
- **Two optional layouts, remembered.** Ambient colour (Settings › Panel, off
  by default) lays a faint field of the selected clip's colour under the list
  and the peek, never while Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast is on.
  ⌘G shows the history as a grid of visual cards with the same date sections,
  ⌘1–9 badges and peek beside it; the arrows move by row and by column.
- **What you type in the Library stays.** A snippet's body survives the
  Library's own refreshes (a finished sync, a new or renamed board) and is
  saved when you move to another snippet or board, the way the title and
  keyword already saved on leaving their field. ⌘S saves in place.

## Also fixed

- The Mac peek masks sensitive titles and hides its hero and derived content
  in Private Mode.
- The board picker takes keyboard focus when opened from the peek or search.
- Returning from a completion window no longer redirects inline editing into
  the search field.
- A row's hover highlight clears while the list scrolls instead of staying on
  a row that moved out from under a still pointer.

## Install or update

Download the [signed DMG](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.9.0/Gancho-0.9.0.dmg)
and its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.9.0/Gancho-0.9.0.dmg.sha256).
Compare the downloaded bytes with the sidecar before installing. Existing direct
installs can use the signed Sparkle update; Homebrew's cask follows the published
artifact. If assets or the update feed are not available yet, publication is not
complete—do not substitute an older candidate from another source.

Gancho requires macOS 15.4 or later; the on-device model tier and Liquid Glass
additionally require macOS 26. The iPhone and iPad targets require iOS 26 or
later and are not distributed by the DMG or Homebrew cask.

## Availability

Local history remains free. Pro is a separate direct purchase and unlocks the
documented higher limits, on-device intelligence, and encrypted iCloud sync.
The App Store and TestFlight channels remain in preparation.

## Known limitations

- Typing a search and pressing Enter before the list refreshes pastes the
  previously highlighted clip; wait for the results to update first.
- A snippet deleted on another device while you are editing it closes the
  editor and drops the unsaved draft.
- This DMG distributes macOS only. iPhone/iPad installation requires a separate
  distribution path; App Store and TestFlight availability is not implied.
