# Gancho v0.11.1 — Windows in front, a quieter idle

Gancho 0.11.1 is a fix release for the Mac. The windows you open from the menu
bar and from notices come up in front of the app you're using, with the
keyboard, and Gancho does less work while it waits in the menu bar.

## Highlights

- **Windows in front.** Settings, Library, Privacy Center, Welcome, Clipboard
  Access, Gancho Pro and the recognized-text review open on top of the app you
  were using and take the keyboard, instead of opening behind it.
- **The screen capture explanation answers to the keyboard.** Return and Escape
  go to it, not to the app you were typing in.
- **A quieter idle.** Gancho follows capture, screen-share, Private Mode and
  menu-bar changes as they happen instead of checking on timers, so it wakes up
  less often at rest. Capture pauses as soon as a screen share starts.
- **Prompts that hold assistive technologies.** The telemetry consent prompt and
  the board picker are modal for VoiceOver, so the panel behind them is neither
  read nor acted on until you answer.
- **Fixes.**
  - An image's Regions and Pick color controls sit below the picture.
  - The source-app filter keeps its list after an interrupted read.
  - Exporting an archive no longer holds every file in memory while it checks
    it.
  - When history can't be saved to disk, the Library and the All clips chip
    still show their counts.

See the complete
[changelog](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
for all changes and fixes.

## Install or update

Download the [signed DMG](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.11.1/Gancho-0.11.1.dmg)
and its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.11.1/Gancho-0.11.1.dmg.sha256).
Compare the downloaded bytes with the sidecar before installing. Existing direct
installs can use the signed Sparkle update; Homebrew's cask follows the published
artifact. If assets or the update feed are not available yet, publication is not
complete—do not substitute an older candidate from another source.

Gancho requires macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon. The iPhone and iPad targets
require iOS 26 or later and are not distributed by the DMG or Homebrew cask.

## Availability

Local history remains free. Pro is a separate direct purchase and unlocks the
documented higher limits, on-device intelligence, and encrypted iCloud sync.
The App Store and TestFlight channels remain in preparation.

## Known limitations

- A clip made with Merge double copies is plain text, so it loses any formatting.
- Image peeks show no scroll bar; the wheel and the trackpad still scroll them.
- Text recognized in images (OCR) stays on the Mac that recognized it; it is
  not synced to your other devices.
- Backups made with Settings › General › Back up history… do not include
  boards, board membership or text recipes yet, and recipes are never synced.
- This DMG distributes macOS only. iPhone/iPad installation requires a separate
  distribution path; App Store and TestFlight availability is not implied.
