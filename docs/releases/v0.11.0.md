# Gancho v0.11.0 — A rebuilt panel, paste runs and agents you can see

Gancho 0.11.0 rebuilds the panel, the peek, the Library, Settings and the iPhone
and iPad apps, and adds new ways to reuse what you copy. It is also the first
release that needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26.

## Highlights

- **A panel that says what it knows.** Search has Exact, Fuzzy, Regex and
  Meaning modes, with `app:`, `type:`, `board:`, `device:` and `before:` filters
  you can save. The paste button names where it pastes, the footer names the
  capture state and its cause, and ⌘J shows what you copied around a result.
- **A peek for each kind of clip.** Colors show HEX, RGB, HSL and Swift values
  with their contrast, images list their dominant colors with an eyedropper, and
  secrets show their mask, how long they have left and a 10-second reveal.
- **Paste runs.** The paste stack checks what already went in and reads "3 of
  5". Skip, Back and Reset move through the run, and a small strip keeps it in
  view with the panel closed.
- **One Transform list.** ⌘T holds the clip's actions, text transforms, recipes
  and on-device Smart paste. A step pipeline shows the text before and what will
  paste after, and "Describe the change…" rewrites text on your Mac with a word
  diff first.
- **Snippets that fill themselves.** `{clipboard}`, `{date}` and `{cursor}`
  placeholders, an Insert placeholder row in the editor, and a Suggested group
  of clips you keep reusing.
- **Optional shortcuts, off by default.** Up next offers the clip you usually
  paste into the app in front, Merge double copies joins two ⌘C presses into one
  clip, Show actions after copying offers a small HUD for colors, links and
  one-time codes, and the Notch shelf takes drops on its boards and image
  actions.
- **Agents you can see.** When a local AI client reads your clips through
  `gancho mcp`, the menu-bar icon shows a dot and a HUD names the client, what it
  read and what it was allowed, with Revoke. "Prepare context for AI…" can now
  share an image's recognized text, or a reviewed copy of its pixels with its
  metadata removed.
- **Privacy you can check.** The Privacy Center opens with "Verify, don't trust",
  Private Mode turns itself off on a timer and says what it skipped, and sharing
  your screen hides previews until you choose otherwise.
- **iPhone and iPad.** A clipboard card names what is on the clipboard without
  reading it, the clip detail is rebuilt, opening an image reads its text on the
  device, and the iPad gets a counted sidebar and drag into the app beside it.
  The Gancho keyboard marks what you inserted and can offer Up next. A large
  widget and a lock-screen banner round it out.

See the complete
[changelog](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## Before you update

- **Intel Macs and macOS 15 stay on 0.10.2.** The Sparkle item and the Homebrew
  cask carry both requirements, so those Macs aren't offered this update.
- **Secrets stop syncing.** Passwords, keys and other secrets stay on the device
  that copied them until they expire. Secrets uploaded by earlier versions are
  deleted from iCloud; the copies on each device stay.
- **Some snippets change meaning.** A snippet that used `{clipboard}`, `{date}`
  or `{cursor}` as a field to type now gets the new placeholder. Other fields are
  unchanged, including those names with a default, such as `{date:today}`.
- **Back up first.** Database migrations run on the first launch and can't be
  undone.

## Install or update

Download the [signed DMG](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.11.0/Gancho-0.11.0.dmg)
and its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.11.0/Gancho-0.11.0.dmg.sha256).
Compare the downloaded bytes with the sidecar before installing. Existing direct
installs can use the signed Sparkle update; Homebrew's cask follows the published
artifact. If assets or the update feed are not available yet, publication is not
complete—do not substitute an older candidate from another source.

Gancho requires macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon. The iPhone and iPad targets
require iOS 26 or later and are not distributed by the DMG or Homebrew cask.

## Availability

Local history remains free. Pro is a separate direct purchase and unlocks the
documented higher limits, on-device intelligence, and encrypted iCloud sync.
The App Store and TestFlight channels remain in preparation.

## Release verification

This note describes a prepared candidate; v0.11.0 is not published until the
tagged release workflow completes and this section records the published
commit, build number, workflow run and DMG checksum.

The release workflow must pass lint, unit tests and both app builds before
packaging. Publication requires Developer ID signing, hardened runtime,
notarization/stapling, Gatekeeper acceptance, the production CloudKit/Push
profile, a SHA-256 sidecar and an EdDSA-signed Sparkle appcast. These automated
checks do not certify physical-device behavior.

This release adds one CloudKit field, the encrypted `parts` on `Clip`, which
holds the boundaries of a merged clip. It must reach the Production schema
before the tag. The signed two-Mac matrix, VoiceOver, screen-OCR
permission/display checks and real iPhone and iPad acceptance remain pending; run
them on the released bytes with synthetic or disposable data and keep backups.
The release captures are 1x renders from the hosted UI tests, which run without
Accessibility, so their main button reads Copy.

## Known limitations

- Image peeks show no scroll bar; the wheel and the trackpad still scroll them.
- Text recognized in images (OCR) stays on the Mac that recognized it; it is
  not synced to your other devices.
- Backups made with Settings › General › Back up history… do not include
  boards, board membership or text recipes yet, and recipes are never synced.
- This DMG distributes macOS only. iPhone/iPad installation requires a separate
  distribution path; App Store and TestFlight availability is not implied.
