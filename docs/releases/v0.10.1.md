# Gancho v0.10.1 — iCloud sync that doesn't get stuck

Gancho 0.10.1 is a maintenance release. In 0.10.0, if the saved data of a
single clip on your Mac could not be read, every iCloud upload stopped behind
it: the panel footer showed a repeating Sync error, the Privacy Center's Recent
issues filled with "Local sync work could not be completed", and nothing reached your
other devices. This release keeps uploads moving around that clip.

## Highlights

- **Uploads keep moving.** A clip whose local data can't be read is skipped
  and stays pending for retry; every other clip uploads. If one stays stuck,
  the footer shows **Waiting to sync** with a count instead of a permanent
  error.
- **Recent issues says how many.** A content-free entry counts the skipped
  clips once, instead of on every 20-second poll. If the local store itself
  fails, Gancho still reports a sync error, so a real storage failure is not
  hidden.
- **Nothing else changes.** The panel, text recipes, meaning suggestions,
  selected context and privacy behavior are the same as in 0.10.0.

See the complete
[changelog](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## Install or update

Download the [signed DMG](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.10.1/Gancho-0.10.1.dmg)
and its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.10.1/Gancho-0.10.1.dmg.sha256).
Compare the downloaded bytes with the sidecar before installing. Existing direct
installs can use the signed Sparkle update; Homebrew's cask follows the published
artifact. If assets or the update feed are not available yet, publication is not
complete—do not substitute an older candidate from another source.

Gancho requires macOS 15.4 or later; the on-device model tier, translation
with installed languages and Liquid Glass additionally require macOS 26. The
iPhone and iPad targets require iOS 26 or later and are not distributed by the
DMG or Homebrew cask.

## Availability

Local history remains free. Pro is a separate direct purchase and unlocks the
documented higher limits, on-device intelligence, and encrypted iCloud sync.
The App Store and TestFlight channels remain in preparation.

## Known limitations

- Why a clip's local data becomes unreadable is still under investigation;
  this release keeps sync moving around such a clip but does not repair it.
- Text recognized in images (OCR) stays on the Mac that recognized it; it is
  not synced to your other devices.
- Backups made with Settings › General › Back up history… do not include
  boards, board membership or text recipes yet, and recipes are never synced.
- This DMG distributes macOS only. iPhone/iPad installation requires a separate
  distribution path; App Store and TestFlight availability is not implied.
