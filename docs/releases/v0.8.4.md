# Gancho v0.8.4 — What leaves Gancho stays private, and nothing gets lost

Gancho 0.8.4 is a maintenance release. It tightens every path that hands a clip
to another app, keeps shared items and iCloud changes from being dropped when
something goes wrong, and stops the history panel from losing your place.

## Highlights

- **Protected clips stay protected on the way out.** JWTs, credit-card numbers
  and secrets are now treated as protected by every outbound path — dragging
  clips out, the menu bar's "Last copied" line, the iPhone and iPad keyboard,
  iOS sharing and filtered exports — even when a clip lost its sensitivity
  flag. Drag-out, the keyboard and sharing re-check the clip right before
  handing it over, so a clip deleted, expired or newly protected in the
  meantime is never delivered.
- **A paste never overrides a password manager's privacy marker.** On iPhone
  and iPad, the paste button, Save Clipboard action, Control Center control and
  keyboard refuse clipboard content marked concealed, transient or
  auto-generated — including when the marker sits on another item of the same
  paste — and "Saved" appears only once the clip is actually stored.
- **Shares and sync changes are no longer dropped.** Items shared from other
  apps leave the queue only after they are durably saved, and iCloud sync only
  advances past a page of changes once it is applied. One unreadable record or
  one clip that cannot be packaged no longer blocks the rest, and a newer local
  edit is never marked as synced by an older upload.
- **The panel keeps your place.** The selected clip — and a multi-selection —
  survives a refresh after a new copy, an edit or a deletion instead of
  jumping back to the first row, so Enter pastes what you chose.

## Also fixed

- iOS sharing sends a clip's full content instead of a truncated preview, and
  images as PNG.
- The iPhone and iPad keyboard lists only clips it can still deliver and says
  when one is no longer available.
- A scrolled history list keeps its loaded rows across a refresh, and a page
  requested during a refresh loads once it finishes.
- An interrupted identity reset after an iCloud account change or a deleted
  zone resumes after a restart instead of leaving stale record identities.

## Install or update

Download the [signed DMG](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.8.4/Gancho-0.8.4.dmg)
and its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/johnny4young/gancho/releases/download/v0.8.4/Gancho-0.8.4.dmg.sha256).
Compare the downloaded bytes with the sidecar before installing. Existing direct
installs can use the signed Sparkle update; Homebrew's cask follows the published
artifact. If assets or the update feed are not available yet, publication is not
complete—do not substitute an older candidate from another source.

Gancho requires macOS 15.4 or later; the on-device model tier additionally
requires macOS 26. The iPhone and iPad targets require iOS 26 or later and are
not distributed by the DMG or Homebrew cask.

## Availability

Local history remains free. Pro is a separate direct purchase and unlocks the
documented higher limits, on-device intelligence, and encrypted iCloud sync.
The App Store and TestFlight channels remain in preparation.

## Known limitations

- Save snippet body edits explicitly before changing Library selection. Draft
  behavior during navigation or sync refresh remains under investigation.
- Typing a search and pressing Enter before the list refreshes pastes the
  previously highlighted clip; wait for the results to update first.
- This DMG distributes macOS only. iPhone/iPad installation requires a separate
  distribution path; App Store and TestFlight availability is not implied.
